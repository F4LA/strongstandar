The Story

Meet Ashley

Ashley is a nurse practitioner and mom of two. After having her kids, her weight climbed to a number she once thought was impossible. She'd already clawed back some ground on her own before ever finding a coach.

Her first coach gave her a single workout to follow for six months. Her second had her dieting and training hard for four months, and the scale never moved. Two coaches, the better part of a year, and she felt like she was starting over from scratch.

As a nurse practitioner who counsels patients on lifestyle change every day, not being able to make it work for herself felt like hypocrisy. What convinced her to try again was a discovery call that didn't hand her another blind plan. It diagnosed what was actually missing: personalization, education, and a real strategy for consistency.

The Turning Point

When It Finally Clicked

She committed to six months built on strategy, education, and accountability, with Coach Jackie, a longtime nurse herself. Jackie built a roadmap that started with habits, not restriction: structured eating, protein at every meal, three days a week of training.

Progress was tracked in weekly averages instead of the daily scale, and real adjustments came every few weeks, built around her real life: an exercise swapped out when it didn't work for her body, a diet break built in around three weeks of travel.

Off days didn't reset the whole week. They got absorbed, and the next day she got back on track.

Looking Back

Ashley's Reflection

Five months with a real plan got her further than a year with two coaches ever did. Training stopped being a chore and became the thing that keeps her anxiety in check, and for the first time, she feels like she's practicing what she's spent years preaching to her patients.
