Her Story

Meet Fraya

For years, Fraya chased different diets and coaches. Each program promised results, but what she received felt impersonal, cookie cutter plans delivered through emails, check ins that never went deeper, and no real support.

"I honestly felt like I threw money away," she admits. When she came across Strong Standard, she was hesitant. But after a genuine 25 minute call with Dr. Joey, something clicked.

"For the first time, someone really asked why I wanted this. I didn't just want another diet, I wanted to take the guesswork out of everything."

That conversation was the start of a turning point.

Where She Started

The Turning Point

When It Finally Clicked

With her coach, Ceci, everything changed. Instead of being handed another rigid plan, Fraya was guided step by step.

They began with one simple, healthy habit, adding more veggies and fruits. From there, they focused on protein, portion awareness, and building workouts she could actually enjoy.

At first, she underestimated the workouts. "Only four moves? That's easy," she thought. But once she started, the real work began. With video feedback on her form, detailed corrections, and personalized adjustments, she discovered a whole new level of training.

Her strength grew. She saw definition in her arms and legs. She lost weight, but more importantly, she gained confidence, both in her body and in her ability to make decisions around food without stress.

For the first time, fitness was not a guessing game. It was a lifestyle she looked forward to.

Looking Back

Fraya's Reflection

This journey was not about chasing perfection or a quick fix. It was about finally finding the support and structure to make lasting progress.

Her advice to anyone still stuck in frustration is simple: stop guessing, and stop trying to do it alone.
